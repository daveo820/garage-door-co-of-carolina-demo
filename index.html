Repairs

Most calls are one of six things.

Joel services and repairs almost every make and model of door and opener. Here is what usually breaks.

Call about a repair
  1. 01Broken springsWhen a spring goes, the door won’t lift. Joel services and replaces just about any spring.
  2. 02CablesShould be checked at least once a year along with the rest of the door.
  3. 03TracksA major part of the door. They need a smooth running surface.
  4. 04Hinges and rollersThey take the brunt of the door’s weight. Keep them aligned and lubricated.
  5. 05Openers and sensorsAlmost every make and model. One reviewer’s fix was a short in the sensor wiring.
  6. 06Scheduled maintenanceMaintenance plans to keep the door rolling.
