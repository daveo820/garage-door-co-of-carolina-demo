# Design DNA: The Garage Door Co. of Carolina

- **Hero archetype:** object led. Copy on the left; on the right a drawn sectional garage door (pure CSS, no image) whose handle is the tap-to-call phone number. New in the portfolio (apex manifesto, acts, all-phases, bb type led job log, absolute edge bleed, cfr engineering report, carroll storefront, core-home editorial split, edgewood scorecard collage, hollingsworth full bleed photo, red3 spec sheet, triple-crown asymmetric panel, wolf magazine cover).
- **Pitch built in:** tap-to-call everywhere. Header button, hero button, door handle, every service row, every band, footer sign, and a sticky call bar on phones (under 760px).
- **Signature motif:** door seams. Three stacked grooves like a sectional panel: in the hero kicker, under every subpage header, the logo mark, the favicon, and the top of the footer (5 uses).
- **Motion:** fade plus 22px rise on `.rv`, once. No blur, no parallax. Reduced motion shows everything.
- **Type:** Archivo 800 (display, wide grotesk) plus Figtree 400 to 700 (body, 18px, 1.62). Self-hosted latin woff2, preloaded. Both new to the portfolio.
